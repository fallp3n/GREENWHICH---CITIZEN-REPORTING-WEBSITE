FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q1

How do I submit a report?

Open the Report page, choose a category, describe the incident, enter the location, and fill in the date and time. Add your details, agree to the privacy notice, then press Submit.

Q2

What evidence can I upload?

Photo or video evidence is optional, but it helps administrators understand the problem. Accepted file types are PNG, JPG, JPEG, and PDF.

Q3

How do I track my report?

After you submit, you receive a report number (for example, SR-20261025-1001). Enter it on the Track page to see the current status of your report.

Q4

What do the report statuses mean?

Submitted: your report was received. Under review: administrators are checking it. Assigned: it was forwarded to the responsible personnel. In progress: the issue is being addressed. Resolved: the issue was handled. Case closed: the case is finalized.
