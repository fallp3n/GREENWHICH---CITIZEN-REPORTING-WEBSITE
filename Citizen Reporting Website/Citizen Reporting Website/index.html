A shared community effort

Who can benefit from community reporting?

Community feedback can help different groups understand e-trike service experiences and identify areas that may need attention.

E-trike Commuters

Passengers can report concerns, share their experiences, and provide suggestions about the services they use.

Drivers and Operators

Drivers and operators can gain a better understanding of passenger feedback and recurring service concerns.

Local Government Units

Local government units may use organized community feedback as an additional reference when reviewing transportation concerns.

Transport Administrators

Personnel responsible for transportation services can use submitted feedback to identify patterns and areas for improvement.
